Field Inventory for the City of Phoenix OHS Community Heat Relief & Resource Request Flow.

Visible label / question Why necessary now? Required? Native control/type Submitted name/value Autocomplete/inputmode Constraint/instruction Sensitivity and minimization decision
Contact full name (required) Identifies the site lead responsible for receiving supply dispatch. Yes <input type="text"> contact_name autocomplete="name" required, maxlength="60" Non-sensitive business contact data.
Organization or site name (required) Confirms the requesting shelter, cooling center, or partner facility. Yes <input type="text"> organization autocomplete="organization" required, maxlength="80" Public facility name only.
Contact email address (required) Primary communication channel for instant automated dispatch confirmation. Yes <input type="email"> email autocomplete="email", inputmode="email" required, aria-describedby="email-help" Email is the sole required contact channel for automated status tracking.
Contact phone number (optional backup) Optional secondary contact method for drivers coordinating onsite arrival. No <input type="tel"> phone autocomplete="tel", inputmode="tel" Optional, aria-describedby="phone-help", pattern="^\d{3}-\d{3}-\d{4}$" Phone is optional to minimize data collection.
Site street address (required) Physical destination for supply drop-off or outreach navigation. Yes <input type="text"> site_address autocomplete="street-address" required, maxlength="120" Facility address only.
Phoenix ZIP code (required) Routes dispatch teams to the correct City of Phoenix OHS service zone. Yes <select> dropdown zip_code autocomplete="postal-code" required, restricted to 10 Phoenix ZIPs Coarse geographic location.
Primary service needed (required) Determines resource allocation (Supplies vs. Onsite Navigation). Yes <input type="radio"> group service_type (supplies | navigation | both) autocomplete="off" required, 3 choices, explicit id/for pairs Single-choice selection prevents conflicting dispatch orders.
Estimated daily people served (required) Calculates required supply volume (water cases, cooling kits). Yes <input type="number"> daily_count inputmode="numeric" required, min="5", max="500", step="5" Bounded range prevents bulk hoarding while accurately sizing requests.
Requested delivery or visit date (required) Establishes arrival schedule for city outreach teams. Yes <input type="date"> request_date autocomplete="off" required, min="2026-11-25" Logistics scheduling date only. Rejects past-date submissions.
Specific site instructions & logistics notes (optional) Captures gate codes, drop-off locations, or parking instructions. No <textarea> site_notes autocomplete="off" Optional, maxlength="300", aria-describedby="notes-help" Optional open field. Help text warns against sensitive client PII.
Send occasional OHS heat-relief program updates Opt-in preference for weather advisories and program news. No <input type="checkbox"> updates (value: yes) autocomplete="off" Optional opt-in checkbox Minimal boolean flag. Defaults to unchecked.
